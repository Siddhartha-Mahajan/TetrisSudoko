## Privacy Friendly Sudoku and LibreSudoku lineage

The Java QQWing port included under
`app/src/main/java/org/secuso/privacyfriendlysudoku/controller/qqwing/` was taken
from the `qqwing` branch of
[Privacy Friendly Sudoku](https://github.com/JFronny/privacy-friendly-sudoku)
at commit `566a298f26838cdb4f033dd681e5ec92d0bf7a9b`.

Its difficulty classification was aligned with
[LibreSudoku](https://github.com/kaajjo/LibreSudoku) at commit
`b04eba5336a43bd3e24a58888a44e85a199f6e8d`, including the mapping used by
this app:

- Easy → QQWing `Easy`
- Medium → QQWing `Moderate`
- Hard → QQWing `Hard`
- Expert → QQWing `Challenge`
